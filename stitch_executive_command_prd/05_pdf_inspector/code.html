11. TERM AND TERMINATION
11.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect until the completion of the Services, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Section 11.

11.2 Coteccons may terminate this Agreement at any time without cause by giving thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the Service Provider. In the event of such termination, Coteccons shall pay the Service Provider for all Services satisfactorily performed up to the date of termination.

12. INDEMNIFICATION
12.1 The Service Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Coteccons and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, or expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees and costs) arising out of or in any way connected with: (a) The Service Provider's access to or use of the Services; (b) The Service Provider's violation of any of the terms of this Agreement; or (c) Any negligent act or omission of Service Provider.

12.2 Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Service Provider's total liability under this Section 12 shall be limited to fifty percent (50%) of the total fees paid under this Agreement, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.
warning Unusual Indemnity Cap

Liability cap is set at 50%, which is significantly below the standard 100% requirement for Tier-1 vendors.

13. CONFIDENTIALITY
13.1 "Confidential Information" means all information, whether oral, written, or in any other form, that is disclosed by Coteccons to the Service Provider in connection with this Agreement. The Service Provider agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of Coteccons.

13.2 The obligations of confidentiality under this Section 13 shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

14. GOVERNING LAW
14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Vietnam. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be resolved by arbitration at the Vietnam International Arbitration Centre (VIAC).
